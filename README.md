# Horologium

**An editorial atlas of daily routines and work habits across history.**

Horologium showcases the documented 24-hour schedules, work rituals, portraits, statistical patterns, and actionable lessons drawn from **250 historical and modern figures**—spanning writers, scientists, philosophers, military leaders, athletes, tech founders, and artists.

## Features

- **Atlas & Filterable Directory** — Search and filter all 250 profiles by discipline, chronotype (*Early Bird*, *Night Owl*, *Biphasic Sleeper*), daily habits, and **Source Fidelity** (*Primary Clocked* vs. *Biographical Record*), or press `/` to jump straight to search.
- **24-Hour Timeline Comparison** — Stack up to three figures side by side along a synchronized 24-hour ruler with a live **NOW** clock indicator, **Shuffle 3** randomizer, and curated comparison trios.
- **Personal Schedule Overlay** — Toggle your own wake, work, and sleep times directly onto the timeline to measure live Deep Work and Sleep overlap against any figure, and export your custom routine as an `.ics` file.
- **Interactive Category Highlighting** — Click any activity swatch in the masthead (*Sleep*, *Deep Work*, *Exercise*, *Leisure*, *Meals*) to isolate that category across the entire archive.
- **Comprehensive Statistics Panel** — 20 live analytical modules covering sleep and deep-work distributions, a 24-hour collective activity curve, Sleep vs. Deep Work correlation (`r`), wake-up and bedtime distributions, deep-work session architecture, wake-to-work latency, Work-to-Sleep ratios, a 24-hour focus & movement density heatmap, habit lift analysis, percentile benchmarks (`P10`–`P90`), Primary Clocked vs. Biographical sensitivity checks, discipline & era matrices, and clickable archive extremes.
- **Lessons Panel** — Eight data-backed principles on focus, sleep, movement, chronotypes, rituals, and recovery distilled directly from the 250-figure statistics, complete with live metrics, actionable rules, and clickable exemplars.
- **Detail Drawer, Portraits & Primary Citations** — Inspect any figure's portrait, exact hourly breakdown, work-ethic quote, historical context, and primary biographical or interview source, with keyboard navigation (`←` / `→`) and URL hash deep-linking.
- **Calendar Export (`.ics`) & Bookmarks** — Generate and download RFC 5545 `.ics` calendar files for Google Calendar or Apple Calendar, and star favorite routines locally in the browser.

## Tech Stack

Built as a single self-contained static site using HTML, Tailwind CSS, Chart.js, and vanilla JavaScript.
